My Journey: The Things That Shape Me

My name is Brenda, and this website is about the things that inspire me, challenge me, and help me grow every day.

I wasn't always interested in technology. My journey began with curiosity and a desire to improve my life and the lives of those around me. Today, I work in IT support while learning Python, full-stack development, cloud technologies, and artificial intelligence. Every day is another step toward becoming an AI Engineer and a Technical Manager.

Chapter 1: The Books That Changed My Thinking

One of the most important habits I have developed is reading.

When I first read Atomic Habits, I learned that success is not usually the result of one big decision. Instead, it comes from small actions repeated consistently over time.

Another book that challenged me was Deep Work. It taught me the importance of focusing deeply on one task without distractions. This lesson has become valuable as I learn programming and technical skills.

The most influential book in my life, however, is the Bible. Through it, I continue to learn about faith, surrender, wisdom, and purpose.

Chapter 2: My Love for Technology

Technology fascinates me because it allows people to solve problems at scale.

When I wrote my first Python program, I realized that coding is like solving a puzzle. Every error message is a clue, and every successful program is proof that persistence pays off.

Today, I enjoy learning:

  • Python
  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Microsoft Intune
  • Cloud Computing

My dream is to build applications that help people save time, make better decisions, and improve their lives.

Chapter 3: My Favorite Foods

Food brings people together, and some of my happiest memories involve sharing meals with family and friends.

Pilau reminds me of celebrations and family gatherings.

Nyama Choma reminds me of weekends and special occasions.

Chapati is one of my comfort foods because it goes well with almost anything.

Ugali and Sukuma Wiki remind me that simple meals can still be satisfying and meaningful.

Chapter 4: The Places I Dream of Visiting

One of my goals is to travel outside Kenya and experience different cultures.

At the top of my travel list is Dublin because I hope to live and work in Ireland for a few years.

I would also like to visit the Cliffs of Moher, explore London, and experience the energy and innovation of Tokyo.

Travel represents growth, learning, and seeing the world from different perspectives.

Chapter 5: My Faith Journey

Faith plays an important role in my life.

Every morning I try to spend time reading Scripture, praying, and reflecting.

One lesson I am learning is surrender—trusting God even when I cannot see the entire path ahead.

Some of my favorite verses include:

  • Proverbs 3:5–6
  • Matthew 6:33
  • Philippians 4:13

These verses remind me to trust God, seek wisdom, and remain faithful during challenges.

Chapter 6: My Goals for the Future

The future I am building includes several important goals:

  1. Become an AI Engineer.
  2. Become a strong Technical Manager.
  3. Build useful software products.
  4. Travel internationally.
  5. Continue growing spiritually.
  6. Achieve financial independence.
  7. Help others learn technology.

Each day I take one small step toward these goals through learning, practicing, and staying consistent.

Chapter 7: A Day in My Life

After work, I spend time studying programming, practicing coding exercises, and learning new technologies.

Some days are difficult. Bugs appear, concepts feel confusing, and progress seems slow.

Yet every completed project, every solved problem, and every new skill reminds me why I started this journey.

I believe that the person I become is shaped by the things I consistently do, the people I learn from, the books I read, and the faith that guides me.

And that is why these are my favorite things.
